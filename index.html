In short: GaltCup keeps everything on your device. It has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network connections. We receive nothing you enter, and no third party receives anything from the app.

1. What the app stores, and where

The readings, temperatures and amounts you type are used only to do the sum on screen and are not saved, except that each Proof calculation is saved to the log (below), automatically unless you set Logging to Manual in Settings, where only Add to log saves it. The app saves, on your own phone, tablet or computer and inside its private storage:

  • Your settings: color, theme, text size, Units (US or Metric), how the Calculate screen takes its numbers (typing or wheels), your chosen correction method and your target Brix, whether the log saves automatically and how many days archived entries are kept, and whether you have seen the guided tour.
  • Any batch sizes you type on the Proof screen, so it can offer them again, and the syrup price per pound and pounds of syrup per year you last typed into the lost-revenue calculators.
  • The proofing log: the Proof calculations saved to it. An entry holds the date and time, the Brix values, the batch size, the water amount, the chart used, the hydrometer reading, syrup temperature and correction method that gave the true Brix, any warnings shown, and any note you type.

There is no account, sign-in or password, and no server of ours that holds any of this, so we cannot see it, recover it, or back it up for you.

2. Permissions

GaltCup asks for none: no camera, photos, location, contacts, microphone or network access. When you create a report, saving or sharing it uses your device’s own save and share screens, which you operate; the app is not given access to your other files.

3. Links that open your browser

Two links open your own browser: the link to this policy on the web (Settings → Privacy), and the www.galtmaple.com link, which appears under the name at the top of every screen and on a report preview. Your browser then makes the connection under its own privacy terms. The app itself makes no network connections and sends nothing to either site. The same text is available inside the app, without a connection, under Settings → Privacy → Privacy policy.

4. Advertising, analytics and third parties

None. The app contains no advertising, no advertising ID, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and no third-party service receives data from it. The open-source libraries it is built from run entirely on your device; their licenses are listed under Settings → Privacy → Open-source licenses.

5. How long it is kept, and deleting it

Everything stays on your device until you delete it. A saved batch size can be removed from the batch-size list on the Proof screen. Deleting a log entry (or all of them) archives it: it leaves the log and the reports but stays on your device, and can be restored from the Archived list on the Log tab, until the number of days set under Settings → Log (35 by default) has passed, and then it is removed for good. You can also delete an archived entry at once. Uninstalling the app removes everything. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

A report you create (from the Log, in the formats and periods the app offers) is a file made on your device. If you choose Save or Share, it goes where you put it through your device’s own save or share screen, such as your Files app, email or a messaging app, and then is under that place’s own terms. The temporary copy made for sharing is in the app’s cache, which your device may clear. GaltCup keeps no other copy and does not send it anywhere itself.

6. Security

Your settings live in the app’s private storage, protected by your device’s own security (screen lock and, where available, device encryption). Because the app has no server and makes no connections, there is no online service to breach.

7. Children

GaltCup is a tool for syrup makers and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age of consent in some countries). It collects no personal information from anyone.

8. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, port or delete. What the app saves is under your control on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it completely.

9. Limits of the application

The syrup temperature GaltCup accepts is 30 to 215°F (-1.1 to 101.7°C in Metric), the coldest and hottest rows printed by any chart. Outside that range the app shows a “Temperature out of range” alert, clears the box and calculates nothing. Each chart is used only within the rows it prints: the GALT Temperature Chart 40 to 215°F (its last row, 209 and up, holds to 215°F), the Cornell 60°F and 68°F charts 30 to 215°F, the Vermont UVM card 60 to 209°F, and the Proofing Chart a Brix reduction of 0.5 to 4. Outside the rows of the chart in use, no correction or water amount is given, and the app says “Outside the chart limits. No correction applied.” GaltCup is a guide, not an official certification.
