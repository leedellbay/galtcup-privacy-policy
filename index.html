Privacy Policy

GaltCup, published by Lee Dell Bay Innovations LLC

Effective November 4, 2026

In short: GaltCup keeps everything on your device. It has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network connections. We receive nothing you enter, and no third party receives anything from the app.

1. What the app stores, and where

The readings, temperatures and amounts you type are used only to do the sum on screen and are not saved. The app saves, on your own phone, tablet or computer and inside its private storage:

  • Your settings: color, theme, text size, your chosen correction method and your target Brix.
  • Any batch sizes you type on the Dilute screen, so it can offer them again.

There is no account, sign-in or password, and no server of ours that holds any of this, so we cannot see it, recover it, or back it up for you.

2. Permissions

GaltCup asks for none: no camera, photos, location, contacts, microphone or network access.

3. The link to this page

Settings → Privacy offers a link to this policy on the web. Tapping it opens your own browser, which then makes the connection under its own privacy terms. The app itself makes no network connections. The same text is available inside the app, without a connection, under Settings → Privacy → Privacy policy.

4. Advertising, analytics and third parties

None. The app contains no advertising, no advertising ID, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and no third-party service receives data from it. The open-source libraries it is built from run entirely on your device; their licenses are listed under Settings → Privacy → Open-source licenses.

5. How long it is kept, and deleting it

Everything stays on your device until you delete it. A saved batch size can be removed from the batch-size list on the Dilute screen, and uninstalling the app removes everything. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

6. Security

Your settings live in the app’s private storage, protected by your device’s own security (screen lock and, where available, device encryption). Because the app has no server and makes no connections, there is no online service to breach.

7. Children

GaltCup is a tool for syrup makers and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age of consent in some countries). It collects no personal information from anyone.

8. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, port or delete. What the app saves is under your control on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it completely.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the effective date above and say so in the app’s release notes.

10. Contact
